About

I am a Professor in the Department of Political Science and Diplomacy at Yonsei University. My research focuses on international political economy, with particular interests in economic sanctions, international development and foreign aid, human rights, and North Korea. I also work on research methodology, including structural estimation, maximum likelihood estimation, game theory, and automated text analysis.

I received my Ph.D. in Political Science from the University of Rochester in 2007. Prior to Yonsei, I held positions at Korea University and Texas A&M University (2007–2012).
